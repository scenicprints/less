# The home screen, as it is today

Read from the Nova backup of 23 November 2026. This is the layout the icons are drawn for and the
layout Less lays out on first run.

Grid: Nova used a 9 by 9 subgrid, which at the sizes used is four across in the pages and five in
the dock. Default page is the middle one.

## Left page

The Google Calendar month widget, the whole page.

## Middle page, the default

- Nudge widget, full width, two rows tall, at the top.
- Row: deck "Home" (Homey, myQ, Nest, Hue, iRobot, Roborock, Google Home), Gmail, Claude, Camera, Photos.
- Row: Paylocity, BodyComp.

## Right page

- Row: deck "Money" (Affirm, Capital One, Citi Mobile, Roost (web), U.S. Bank, Venmo, Wealthfront, Chase, Discover, PayPal, Robinhood, Synchrony), Amazon Shopping, Maps, Calculator.
- Row: Pantry, YouTube, Instagram.
- Row: Reddit, WhatsApp, Snapchat, Poppy.
- Row: deck "Bambu" (Bambu Handy, CyberBrick), deck "Travel" (Airbnb, Hostelworld, Uber, Waymo), FuelWise.
- Row: Milepost, Foos, Juno, Fluidez. These four are web apps pinned through Brave, not installed
  apps; they arrive as Brave shortcuts and keep the icon the site provides.

## Dock

Phone, Messages, Brave, Apple Music, Workouts.

## Drawer groups Nova had

Media, Money, Food, Games, Shopping, Social, Travel. Less has no groups in the drawer; they are
listed here only so nothing is lost.
